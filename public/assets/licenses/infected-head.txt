Putrid Swarm — infected head credits

The eight human head models include adapted geometry, skin and normal-map data from
Infinite, 3D Head Scan by Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite-Realities, based on work at
https://www.triplegangers.com/ and licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Morgan McGuire and Guedis Cardenas prepared the distributed scan.
https://casual-effects.com/data/

The source for this adaptation is the r170 Three.js example asset set.
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r170/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith

Putrid Swarm cropped and retopologized the bust for two viewing distances, opened and
resculpted its eyelids, fitted original face studies, modified the scan's surface maps
and shaped and dressed each character. The portraits, hair, wardrobe and animal art
were created for the game.
