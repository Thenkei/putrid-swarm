# Infected source art

The original portraits, animal profiles and material swatches were generated for Putrid Swarm. The scan of the head, its diffuse map and its tangent-space normal map are by Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite-Realities. Morgan McGuire and Guedis Cardenas prepared the distribution. The source assets here are from Three.js r170. [Source](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r170/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith) · [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) · [Full in-game attribution](../../public/assets/licenses/infected-head.txt)

Run `pnpm exec tsx tools/bake-infected.ts` to regenerate the character textures and both head meshes offline. The head bake fits the original portraits to the scan and its modified eyes. The source studies of canvas, denim, leather and directional fur supply the surface grain for uniforms, equipment and wildlife.
